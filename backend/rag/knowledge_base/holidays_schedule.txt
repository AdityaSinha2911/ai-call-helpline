College Holiday Schedule

Public Holidays:
The college remains closed on all national holidays including Republic Day, Independence Day, and Gandhi Jayanti.
Diwali holidays are typically for 5 days as per the academic calendar.
Holi holiday is observed for 2 days.

Semester Breaks:
Winter break is usually from late December to early January.
Summer break follows the end of the May examination period.
The exact holiday schedule is published on the college website at the start of each academic year.

Academic Calendar:
Students are advised to check the academic calendar on the ERP portal for exact holiday dates.
Any changes to the holiday schedule are announced via notice boards and the college website.
