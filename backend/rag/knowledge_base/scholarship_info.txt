Scholarship Information

Merit-cum-Means Scholarship:
Students with annual family income below Rs 2.5 lakh are eligible.
Minimum academic score required is 60 percent in the previous year.
Application window opens every August and closes on September 30th.
Documents required: income certificate, previous year marksheet, Aadhaar card, bank passbook.
Submit applications at the Scholarship Cell, Room 104, Admin Block.
Contact: scholarship@college.edu or extension 205.

State Government Scholarship:
Apply directly on the state government scholarship portal.
College scholarship cell assists with verification of documents.
Processing takes 30 working days after submission.
