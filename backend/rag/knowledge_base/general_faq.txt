College Helpline General Information

Office Hours:
The administrative office is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
On Sundays and public holidays the office remains closed.

Library:
The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays.
Saturday library hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Library is closed on Sundays.

ID Card:
Lost ID cards can be replaced at the administrative office.
A fee of Rs 200 is charged for ID card replacement.
Bring a passport-size photograph and fill Form ADM-04.

Bonafide Certificate:
Bonafide certificates are issued within 3 working days.
Apply at the administrative counter with your registration number.
