Anti-Ragging Policy

Zero Tolerance:
The college maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against ragging.
Any form of ragging is a criminal offense punishable under law.
Complaints can be reported to the Anti-Ragging Committee.

Reporting:
Students can report ragging incidents by calling the Anti-Ragging helpline.
Anonymous complaints are also accepted at the administrative office.
Action is taken within 24 hours of receiving a complaint.

Punishment:
Students found guilty of ragging face immediate suspension or expulsion.
A First Information Report (FIR) may be filed with the police.
