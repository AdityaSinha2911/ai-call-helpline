Hostel Rules and Regulations

Entry and Exit:
Hostel gate closes at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends.
Students must carry their hostel ID at all times.
Visitors are allowed only in the visitor's lounge between 10 AM and 7 PM.

Mess Timings:
Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM
Lunch: 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM
Dinner: 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM
No food is served outside mess hours.

Complaints:
Hostel maintenance complaints can be registered by calling this helpline.
Complaints are forwarded to the hostel warden within 24 hours.
Emergency maintenance issues contact warden directly at extension 301.

Fees:
Hostel fee is due on the 10th of every month.
Late payment attracts a fine of Rs 100 per day after a 5-day grace period.
