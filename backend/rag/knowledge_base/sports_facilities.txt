Sports Facilities

Available Sports:
The college has facilities for cricket, football, basketball, badminton, volleyball, and table tennis.
An Olympic-size swimming pool is available for students.
The sports ground is open from 6 AM to 8 PM daily.

Gymnasium:
The college gymnasium is equipped with modern fitness equipment.
Gymnasium is open from 6 AM to 9 PM on all working days.
Students need to show their ID card to access the gymnasium.

Physical Education:
Physical education classes are part of the curriculum for first and second year students.
Inter-college sports tournaments are organized every year.
Students can join college sports teams by contacting the Sports Cell.
