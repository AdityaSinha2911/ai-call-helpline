Fee Payment Policy

Payment Schedule:
Semester fees must be paid within the first 15 days of each semester.
Late payment results in a fine of Rs 50 per day.
After 30 days of non-payment the student's exam form may be withheld.

Payment Methods:
Fees can be paid online through the college ERP portal.
Cash payments accepted at the accounts office between 10 AM and 3 PM.
Demand drafts must be in favor of the college principal.

Fee Receipts:
Official receipts are generated automatically after online payment.
For cash payments receipts are issued at the accounts counter.
Keep fee receipts safe as duplicates require a formal application.

Refund Policy:
Fee refunds are processed within 30 working days of application.
Refund applications must be submitted to the accounts office with original receipts.
